# nvgfx: 2026-10-30 (full run, window 2026-10-29 10:04 UTC to 2026-10-30)

NullMoth shipped v2.6.4 (translator work only). The window drag thread got its first stock 2.6.3 number.

## Actionable

**[#356](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/356#issuecomment-6495194027), drag rate on stock
2.6.3.** karma1228 (RTX 3060 Laptop, 1920x1080 165 Hz) counted 508 and 384 flips in two 8 s drags of a large
window: 63.5 and 48 per second, with the GPU at 8 to 12 percent. Our 145 to 148 per second came from a scripted
800x500 Finder drag on our fork of 2.4.3, so the two are not directly comparable. Their new issue
[#456](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/456) puts WindowServer at 13 to 14 ms of CPU per
presented frame, with the time in descriptor set allocation and update, the same profile as in #356. High confidence on
the numbers, Medium on the cause.

**[#455](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/455), scaled HiDPI modes still missing on 2.6.4.**
Sequoia 15.8.1, RTX 4060 Laptop, 2560x1600 240 Hz. The log validates the 1680x1050 scaled mode, but Displays does not
list it. Our fix for #354 went out in 2.6.3 (PR #421) and was tested on Tahoe with a 12-byte override. Whether this is
a Sequoia gap or a different override format is unverified.

## Notable

- [v2.6.4](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/releases/tag/v2.6.4): texture arrays, sampler rewrites and
  a WebGL shadow fix in the translator. Nothing in the drag, hold or HiDPI paths.
- [#454](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/454): on an RTX 5080, WindowServer spins in NVRM when
  NVKMS rejects a modeset during a reconnect of an external DSC monitor. Our single eDP panel is unlikely to hit it.
- [#429](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/429): a 300 Hz eDP DSC panel goes black. Our panel is
  165 Hz without DSC.
- New related project: [zzzyy678/SMCNvidiaGPU](https://github.com/zzzyy678/SMCNvidiaGPU) (MIT), a VirtualSMC plugin
  that reads NVIDIA temperature, power, fans and clocks on NullMoth 2.6.x through a table of NVRM offsets per build. It
  overlaps our `nvgfx-smi`; our builds would need their own table.
