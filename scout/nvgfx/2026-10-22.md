# NVIDIA on macOS: what moved, 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-22

Target: [NvGfx](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/NvGfx), our NullMoth fork for a Legion 5 15IAX10 (RTX 5070 Laptop, GB206, 165 Hz eDP panel on the NVIDIA GPU, macOS 26.7.1 25G241). The fork's default branch is `nvgfx-2.4.3`. Confidence uses High / Medium / Low / Unverified.

## Actionable

**NullMoth 2.4.4 fixes a hang reported on our exact machine** (High). Upstream [#322](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/322): Terminal and iTerm2 hang after `vk: completion witness missing`, reported on a Legion 5 15IAX10 with the RTX 5070 Laptop (10DE:2D18) at 3360x2100 165 Hz on 25G241. The fix is in [2.4.4](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/releases/tag/v2.4.4). The fork is on 2.4.3, so it doesn't have it yet.

**Opt-in flip latch for high refresh panels** (High that it exists, Unverified on our panel). [2.5.2](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/releases/tag/v2.5.2) adds `debug.nvrmfb_flip_latch=2` (upstream [#300](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/300)). The reporter measured the per-submit cost fall from 3.14 ms to 0.22 ms on a 240 Hz panel. It's a sysctl, so it can be tried on 2.5.x without a rebuild. The default stays at 1 because it flickered on some machines.

**Two upstream routes for Discord's lag (our I-071)** (Medium).
- [2.5.1](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/releases/tag/v2.5.1) uploads CPU-drawn surfaces from the IOSurface's own pages with the copy engine (upstream #296). It overlaps the fork's async upload at bind time, and the two may stack: theirs removes the page-fault cost, ours removes the wait. `NVMTL_ASYNC_TEX_UPLOAD=1` (off by default) makes texture uploads async too.
- Open PR [#348](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/348) puts Discord on ANGLE Metal instead of GL, which skips Apple's GL-on-Metal entirely. `NVMTL_ANGLE_METAL=1` does the same per app today. Upstream reverted Electron on ANGLE Metal once before over corruption on RTX 30/50 cards, so test it on GB206 first.

**Move the fork to 2.5.3, as a staged port** (Medium).
- [2.5.0](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/releases/tag/v2.5.0) moves the NVIDIA base from 610.57.04 to 615.78.08 and puts gamma on 615's output-LUT surface. The fork's NVRM changes (power-source refresh, brightness) will conflict.
- 2.5.0 to 2.5.2 panic at boot when any `nvregdwords=` boot-arg is set; [2.5.3](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/releases/tag/v2.5.3) fixes it.
- The fork's boot-hold cap is now upstream as `nvrmholdcap` (merged in 2.5.2, with authorship kept), so that change can be dropped.
- Other gains: the 2.4.3 fix for the `thread_call group 'high' reached max thread cap` panic, 2.4.4's earlier Metal device on laptops (about 8 s instead of 60 s), and the window-drag app kills fixed in 2.5.1.
- Licence: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, same as the fork.

**Brightness: compare before porting** (Medium). Open PR [#312](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/pull/312) keeps 0 to 65535 logical brightness targets across power requests and delays the NVRAM save; NullMoth said it will merge once panels are checked. Diff it against the fork's own brightness handling before the port.

## Notable

- **Work from our side landed upstream** (High). The submit-ahead pass ([PR 289](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/pull/289)) shipped in 2.3.7, the AIR digest change in 2.4.3, table-called functions in 2.4.4 and the boot-hold cap in 2.5.2. NvGfx [#6](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/NvGfx/issues/6) can likely close after a re-measure.
- **Scaled modes** (High). 2.5.1 moves scaled modes to the head scaler (#273), relevant to NvGfx [#5](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/NvGfx/issues/5). HiDPI above half native ([#341](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/341)) is still open upstream.
- **Early slow drags** (Medium). Nothing upstream covers NvGfx [#11](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/NvGfx/issues/11). The fork's change that runs the ahead pass on its own queue is still fork-only and could be offered upstream.
- **Another RTX 5070 laptop on NullMoth** (High that it exists; ideas only, no licence). [BelikPePe-UA/legion5-15irx10-hackintosh](https://github.com/BelikPePe-UA/legion5-15irx10-hackintosh) runs 2.4.3 on a Legion 5 15IRX10.
  - A one-byte patch raises NVMTL's 32 MiB staging-buffer threshold, because a 3840x2400 HiDPI surface is 36.9 MB. WindowServer CPU fell from about 108% to 46%.
  - It also patches NVRM's viewport scale for HiDPI.
  - Worth checking whether the fork's 2560x1600 HiDPI modes cross the same threshold.
- **HP OMEN 16 on Tahoe** (High). [Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26](https://github.com/Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26) (MIT) runs 2.5.2 with an RTX 5070 Laptop and uses `nvrmacpibl=1` for brightness.
- **NullMoth's installer bundles four of our kexts** (High). [nullmoth/1401](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401) commit [bba6075de](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401/commit/bba6075de) pins LegionCbmrFix, LegionCpuPower, LegionI2CArrowLake and LegionITEKbdFix by hash and credits them correctly in its NOTICE. It still reports MT7925 as having no driver.
- **Freezes after login on 2.4.3 and 2.5.2** (Low). Upstream [#328](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/328), on another Legion, is still open.
- **metal2vulkan on ANV** (Medium). [steelbrain/reims-vgpu](https://github.com/steelbrain/reims-vgpu) moved its translator to builds that cut ANV cold compiles from about 14 s to 4 s and size oversized pipelines to fit ANV. The translator shares a base with ours; this matters to the `xe` branch.

## Noise

NVIDIA open-gpu-kernel-modules 615.71.10 and 580.196.07 are datacenter releases with nothing for GB206 or laptops. `nullmoth/1401-probe` only renamed a script comment. Five discovery hits (an EFI builder, an Apple Silicon Triton backend, a game port, a G14 EFI, an OpenCore audit skill) don't touch this project.
