# nvgfx: 2026-11-14 to 2026-11-21

Read from GitHub only. Nothing below is reproduced on our machine.

- **NullMoth 3.0 is out (2026-11-14) and is the only release after 2.9.** No commits on main since. The release fixes processes losing the GPU right after login on 2.9, the System Settings Displays hang, Photo Booth, WindowServer kernel time during video and a shutdown hang ([v3.0](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/releases/tag/v3.0)). The diff from 2.9 is 81 commits and 203 files, mostly additions (legacy macOS kits, GL, docs and CI), with 12 files in NVRM and 3 in NVRMFB, so our rebase should be low to medium work. Nothing in the notes is specific to RTX 50 laptops. Confidence: High for the release content, Low for "no RTX 50 effect" because the NVRM diff was not read line by line.
- **Its hybrid-panel fix is on main but not in the 3.0 package.** A large part of 3.0 is about panels wired to the Intel GPU, including the boot panic fix from [#555](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/555). A tester in that thread found the package still ships the 2.9 NMIntelFB binary and the installer does not install it. Built from main it no longer panics, but the panel stays black and no frames reach the head. That is the same wiring our laptop can boot with, so a rebase should build NMIntelFB from source, and we should compare results. Confidence: Medium, one tester.
- **Decode, HDR and sleep on RTX 50 are still open upstream.** Reports without an answer: Apple TV stops after 10 seconds on an RTX 5070 Laptop ([#612](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/612)), S3 resume loses the GPU on a 5070 Ti ([#627](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/627)), AV1 and VP9 ([#630](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/630)), HDR ([#625](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/625)), sleep on laptops ([#618](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/618)). Nothing to take. It confirms our own list.
- **NVIDIA open modules 620.73.04** (2026-11-18) is one commit over 620.70.06: DisplayPort handling for DP++ HDMI adapters and MST, flip and colour pipeline changes, and firmware for a chip that is not ours ([release](https://github.com/NVIDIA/open-gpu-kernel-modules/releases/tag/620.73.04)). It is the tag to pin when we rebuild NVRM on 620.
- **An idle power number from a hybrid Dell.** With `BOOST=0` a G15 5530 (RTX 4050) measured about 12 W less at idle on battery ([#428](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/428)). One machine, their measurement. Worth trying on ours.
- **New: HighDelay's NVK fork of NullMoth** ([repo](https://github.com/HighDelay/nvidia-macos-driver-nvk), 3.0.2-nvk on 2026-11-17). Commit titles mention Safari hardware decoding and laptop panel brightness recovery, and [#621](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/621) reports its codec registration failing on one card. Only titles read. Ideas only. Confidence: Low.
- **A ROG Strix SCAR 18 (Core Ultra 9 290HX Plus, RTX 5090 Laptop) runs 3.0 and reports working panel backlight with `nvrmacpibl=1`**, and a reboot where sleep should be ([repo](https://github.com/saeckl/ROG-Strix-SCAR18-G835LXG-macOS)). Same CPU family as ours. Their report.

Skipped: AMD and Ryzen threads, legacy card support, and four discovery hits that are EFI folders.
