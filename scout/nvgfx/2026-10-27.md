## Actionable

**Our drag fix, measured on an RTX 5080, and one regression in it.**
[nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver#412](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/412): a user took six of
our NvGfx plugin commits, rebased them onto NullMoth 2.6.2 and measured window drags on an RTX 5080. Stock 2.6.2 gave
85 to 100 flips/s with stalls of 63 to 124 ms; with our commits the stalls go away. That is the first measurement of the
I-069 fix on hardware other than ours. They held back two commits, the ones that cache `setRenderPipelineState` and the
sampler variant, because with them Alacritty (OpenGL) text jumped backward. Two more did not apply cleanly (the System
Settings icon speedup and the Chrome CPU-raster scroll). We need to check the two state-cache commits against an
OpenGL terminal before we call them done. High confidence: their own numbers, our code.

**Brightness that survives a restart.** [PR #312](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/pull/312) keeps a
logical brightness target from 0 to 65535 across power requests and maps it to 5 to 100 percent on the panel. The
submitter now reports the saved level comes back after a restart on an RTX 5080 Laptop under macOS 26.7. Still open and
unreviewed. NullMoth has no licence, so we would rewrite the idea, not copy it. Medium confidence (one machine).

## Notable

**HDR is blocked by the class name, not the pixel format.** In
[#341](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/341) a user traced CoreDisplay's vendor check: a
framebuffer class whose name contains "NVDA" is treated as vendor 8 and gets `force8BPC = YES`, so 10-bit and HDR
timings are never considered. Advertising 10 bpc, BT.2020 and HDR10 did not change `SupportsHDRMode`. Renaming the class
cleared the flag but WindowServer then lost its Metal compositor and aborted. This explains why our 30-bit attempts
(N-187 to N-190) could not get further. Medium confidence, one report, not reproduced by us.

**HiDPI: the missing flag and the override format.** In
[#354](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/354) the fix we pointed at was confirmed:
`getInformationForDisplayMode()` sets only Valid, Safe and NotPreset, and scaled modes need
`kDisplayModeValidForHiResFlag` (0x00800000) to reach the public list. In #341 and
[#360](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/360), independent reports on GA106 confirm that 12-byte
`scale-resolutions` entries with backing sizes work, 16-byte entries carrying flag 0x00200000 hide the modes, and
scales above about 1.3x at 165 Hz hit a scaler bandwidth limit. A desktop RTX 3060 at 120 Hz over DisplayPort shows the
same refusal. Matches our I-059 notes. Medium confidence, not merged upstream.

**The shader cache cost is a cold-cache cost.** The reporter of
[#370](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/370) measured the translator's cache and found the
`writeToFile`/`fsync` storm only happens while the cache fills after a driver update. So it comes back after every
update. That supports moving the write off the hot path, which is what our cache-write-async branch does. High
confidence on the measurement.

**Releases 2.5.5 to 2.6.2.** [2.5.5](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/releases) fixes Discord video,
encoder keyframes, Screen Sharing (buffer-backed textures accept any view format) and ANGLE Metal in Brave and Edge.
2.6.0 makes a driver update need one restart; 2.6.1 and 2.6.2 are the same driver with installer fixes. Read from the
release notes and commit titles only.

**A frame missed every 0.6 s.** [#410](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/410) reports every app
missing a frame at a steady 0.6 s rhythm with WindowServer blocked in the kernel at the same rhythm. A fix PR is
planned. Worth watching next to our drag work. Unverified.

**mickys/nvidia-macos-driver** ([2.5.4-mickys.1](https://github.com/mickys/nvidia-macos-driver)): thirteen `games/*`
branches with translator fixes (phi on local fields, gather-compare, cube-as-array, struct-member arrays), presenting
drawables on a serial queue, and 70 Steam games tested on a 4090. The present-queue branch sits close to our drag work.
