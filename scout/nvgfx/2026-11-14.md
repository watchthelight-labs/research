# nvgfx: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-14

Quick run after our boot N-374 (NvGfx 2.9-nvgfx.1 trial). Read from GitHub; what we measured ourselves is marked.

- **NullMoth 3.0 is out.** [v3.0](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/releases/tag/v3.0) was published on 2026-11-14 for the driver, with [1401 v3.0](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401/releases/tag/v3.0) beside it. 82 commits since 2.9. Confidence: High (release page and tag).
- **A 2.9 fault that matters to anyone on 2.9.** The release notes say processes could lose the GPU right after login on 2.9 (transparent Dock, missing icons, a lock screen that crashes in a loop, apps crashing at launch): the GPU could read a small command before the driver's write was visible to it, and stopped the process's channel. 3.0 no longer lets the GPU cache that memory. Reports: [#564](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/564). On our 2.9 trial boot we have not seen it in the first quarter of an hour (measured: no crash reports, Dock normal), which proves nothing about later logins. Confidence: Medium (upstream's own description, not reproduced by us).
- **Backlight after a restart ([#582](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/582)).** Fixed by [e910c69a](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/commit/e910c69a): the cause is firmware or an OpenCore setup that drops NVRAM writes, so `nullmoth-backlight` comes back stale and the panel starts at full brightness. The installer adds a launch daemon that copies the variable to disk every 30 s and at shutdown, and restores it through a new root-only sysctl `debug.nvrmfb_backlight_restore` only when the two disagree. On our machine the variable reads `02 ff ff` after the N-374 restart; whether that is a stale value or simply full brightness is not settled (our I-137). Confidence: High for the upstream change, Unverified for our case.
- **Other 3.0 fixes we can use.** Displays settings pane hanging when it opens ([#567](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/567)), black camera previews (4:2:2 video textures on by default, [#568](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/568); [#585](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/585) says Photo Booth is still blank on 3.0), WindowServer kernel time during video ([#566](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/566): a surface is marked once per commit), a shutdown that hung on a GPU usage reading, and a monitor that came up as a 1080p television after some restarts. The installer now adds `gfxrst=4` when WhateverGreen is present and keeps override HiDPI sizes ([ef5ca5bd](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/commit/ef5ca5bd)). Confidence: High that the changes exist, Unverified on our hardware.
- **Hybrid panel work.** NMIntelFB no longer leaves published state behind after a failed start ([#555](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/555), [7a8141d5](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/commit/7a8141d5)), and the hybrid panel maps before it publishes. Relevant to our Hybrid boots, where NMIntelFB 0.3 is loaded but switched off.
- **Developer kit.** Upstream added a setup guide, tests on every pull request, issue templates and a sign-off rule ([5289b350](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/commit/5289b350)). Pull requests from us must follow it from now on.
- **1401's community catalogue.** [d77fc539](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401/commit/d77fc539) adds 27 integrated-GPU projects that claim Tahoe. WTLIntelGfx and our NVIDIA fork stay listed as "reviewed for merge"; WTLIntelGfx is still named in the build notice.
- **Still open upstream and close to our hardware:** HDMI plugged in after boot gets no connect interrupt ([#581](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/581)), small-BAR1 fallback never brings the display up ([#489](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/489)), Blender Cycles lists no Metal GPU ([#586](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/586)).

## What we do with it
Our fork moves from 2.9 to 3.0 before anything is released: 2.9-nvgfx.1 stays a local trial build. Our own measurement on 2.9 that upstream may want: window drags hold about 90 flips per second while WindowServer is still translating cold shaders after login, where the old slow phase gave 4 to 26. The steady rate itself is lower than the 147 to 148 we measured in October, and we do not know why yet.
