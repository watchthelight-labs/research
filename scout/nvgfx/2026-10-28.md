## Notable

**NMIntelFB brightness** ([41e7e68](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/commit/41e7e6801a)): panel
brightness through the PCH PWM, with WhateverXe's licence added. NMIntelFB still publishes no `VRAM,totalMB`, so it
shows the 15 MB framebuffer as VRAM; we fixed that in XeGfxIntelFB 0.6 and have an upstream-ready patch. Medium
confidence.

**#424** ([issue](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/424)): frame submission not locked to vblank at
165 Hz on an RTX 4060 Laptop, a drop about every 2 s; the reporter suggests `nvhwvbl=1` but could not test it. Our panel
is also 165 Hz. Worth a test on the 5070 before answering. Medium confidence.

**#426** ([issue](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/426)): an RTX 5050 Laptop (GB207) user on
26.7.2 hits a translator bail-out on a sampler array of 2 in three.js. Not tested here yet. Medium confidence.

**2.6.3 conf precedence**: a setting in the user's own block of nvrm610.conf now wins over the shipped value (release
notes, with #368 and #399). Our I-085 (knobs lost after guard rollbacks) came from the other order, so this may let us
drop the workaround once the fork is on 2.6.3. Medium confidence until tested.
