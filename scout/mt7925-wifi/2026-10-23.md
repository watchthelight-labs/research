# MediaTek MT7925 Wi-Fi: what moved, 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-23

## A second MT7925 machine wrote up a day with our kexts

[Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26](https://github.com/Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26) (MIT, in Chinese) is an HP OMEN 16-ap1xxx: Ryzen 9 9955HX, RTX 5070 Laptop, MT7925 Wi-Fi `14C3:7925`, Bluetooth `0E8D:8C38`, macOS 26.7.1 (25G241), OpenCore 1.0.8 from NullMoth's 1401 app. It runs MtkWlan 0.11.3, MtkWlanNative 0.4.11, MtkBt 0.2.4, BlueToolFixup 2.7.2 and LegionBtFix 0.2.0 with `mtkfe=native mtknativeif=1`, kext order and MinKernel as in our READMEs. The firmware has no S3, so nothing there covers sleep. Seventeen commits on 2026-10-23 added a pitfall table and a version section; the parts that are feedback on our drivers:

- **Saved network stops joining** ([14581e5c](https://github.com/Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26/commit/14581e5c), README line 634): a previously joined network spins and fails; Forget and rejoin fixes it, sometimes with Private Wi-Fi Address switched off for that network. New report, no logs; the private-address lever points at the `set_mac` path. Medium.
- **Slow scans and intermittent DFS networks** (same commit, lines 637 and 813): they read our `XZ` country code correctly. `setCOUNTRY_CODE` is recorded only ([src/MtkWlanNative.cpp](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLWifiMenu/blob/main/src/MtkWlanNative.cpp) 439-455) and the core always runs the US domain with 52-144 passive at a 120 ms dwell ([src/MtkScan.cpp](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLMtkWireless/blob/main/src/MtkScan.cpp) 489-546), so an AP on a DFS channel is caught at most once per sweep. Their workaround, pin the router to 36-48 or 149-161, is right for a US domain. The `XZ` fact was in our README; the scan effect was not. High on the code, Medium on the effect.
- **Instant Hotspot entry does nothing** ([c8f770bd](https://github.com/Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26/commit/c8f770bd), line 636): the Continuity hotspot entry in the Wi-Fi menu does not react; joining the same SSID through Other... or `networksetup` with the password works (iPhone on Maximize Compatibility, 2.4 GHz WPA2). They blame AWDL; discovery of that entry is BLE, so the cause is unverified (itlwm had the same symptom, [#491](https://github.com/OpenIntelWireless/itlwm/issues/491)). If that manual join was WPA2-PSK, it is the first on-air WPA2 join through the native front end; we asked them to confirm.
- **Bluetooth** ([14f8e5c1](https://github.com/Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26/commit/14f8e5c1), lines 635 and 764-815): a third tested id, `0E8D:8C38`, on 0.2.4: `MtkBtState` ready, 3 sections, 447,872 bytes, On as THIRD_PARTY_DONGLE, with LegionBtFix on a non-Lenovo machine. On 0.2.2 Bluetooth switched itself off and on, with `bluetoothd` crash reports showing SIGBUS in `TxLoop`. Their attribution ("the 0.2.2 reset loop") is off: the reset loop was 0.2.3's and 0.2.4 fixed it; 0.2.2's bug was the warm-restart half-load. The SIGBUS site is new and not the LE-connect crash LegionBtFix patches; the crash report is not in the repo. Symptom High, cause Unverified.
- **AirDrop feasibility** ([docs/airdrop-feasibility.md](https://github.com/Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26/blob/main/docs/airdrop-feasibility.md)): desk research that quotes our native #2. Useful reference list: [Owly](https://github.com/t4t5/owly) (MIT, sans-IO AWDL state machine, MT7925 is its first supported card on Linux), [mt76-usb-macos](https://github.com/Network-Weather/mt76-usb-macos) (Clear BSD, user-space MT7921U/MT7925U over libusb with experimental injection), [OWL](https://github.com/seemoo-lab/owl) (GPL-3.0). Their effort estimates are guesses; their affected-feature table overstates (Handoff and Watch unlock are BLE). Low beyond the references.

Not about our kexts but worth knowing: NullMoth 2.5.2 keeps no brightness across boots and the panel jumps to maximum a few hundred ms after a display wake; their MIT `tools/brightness` watchdog holds it down, and upstream [PR #312](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/pull/312) is the real fix. 1401's USB map writer keys ports by class name, so a second port on the same controller overwrites the first (README section 15); no upstream issue exists for that.

## Elsewhere in the window

- **Apple's own MediaTek driver.** macOS 26 ships `com.apple.AppleSunriseWLAN.dext` ([ipsw-diffs, 26.6.2 to 26.7](https://github.com/blacktop/ipsw-diffs/blob/8af66bf3a924930979237d896ee447f0f2684ac8/macOS/26_6_2_25G83_vs_26_7_25G220/MACHOS/filesystem/System/Library/DriverExtensions/com.apple.AppleSunriseWLAN.dext/com.apple.AppleSunriseWLAN.md)). On tonymacx86 ([thread 332855](https://www.tonymacx86.com/threads/332855/)) a kext port of it runs three MT7922 cards on 25G241 with SIP on (two users); MT7925 is "planned". What the dext matches and whether it can drive an MT7925 are unverified; the port is a binary built from Apple's code, so nothing there is usable as code.
- **[eiyuumiru/SimpleMtkWlan v0.3.0-beta](https://github.com/eiyuumiru/SimpleMtkWlan/releases/tag/v0.3.0-beta)** (GPL-2.0, ideas only), RZ616 on 15.8.1 and 26.7.1: beacon-loss events (0x13) were discarded, so after the AP went away the interface stayed joined with the link up ([a69355d2](https://github.com/eiyuumiru/SimpleMtkWlan/commit/a69355d2)); deep sleep was switched on before RUN and joins to fast APs timed out ([81e4232](https://github.com/eiyuumiru/SimpleMtkWlan/commit/81e4232a)); cacheable bounce buffers took throughput from 41 to 96 Mb/s ([9eff5442](https://github.com/eiyuumiru/SimpleMtkWlan/commit/9eff5442)). The first is a candidate cause to check for our traffic stall after about 38 minutes. Medium.
- **MT7922 and RZ616 options for testers** ([#2](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLMtkWireless/issues/2)): [YYavicWiFi](https://github.com/yusufcanyavic/YYavicWiFi) (14c3:7922/7961/0608, native menu on 15.8.1 only, author-measured 913 Mb/s), [AirportMtkWlan-RZ616](https://github.com/lincyppq/AirportMtkWlan-RZ616) (14c3:0616 on Tahoe, source only, unproven), and three SimpleMtkWlan forks adding 14c3:0616 in two days. None uses our code.
- **Firmware**: linux-firmware updated the MT7925 Wi-Fi pair on 2026-10-20 ([5a3f625f](https://gitlab.com/kernel-firmware/linux-firmware/-/commit/5a3f625f)). Not tried.
