## mt76: keep the RX PN state of a station across a rekey (actionable, Medium)

[openwrt/mt76 10983a4b](https://github.com/openwrt/mt76/commit/10983a4b) (Felix Fietkau, mt7925/main.c, +3 -3).
At a PTK rekey mac80211 removes the old key before it sets the new one. `mt7925_set_link_key()` cleared
`rx_check_pn` at the removal, so until the new key arrived `mt76_check_ccmp_pn()` skipped the replay check for frames
the hardware still decrypts. The fix keeps `rx_check_pn` of the station until its next key, as mt7996 does.

What it means for us: it sits next to the group key PN per TID work (mt76 339bbc19, not ported yet). Our WTLWireless
does its own key handling, so the same gap may exist at a rekey: a window with no replay check between removing the old
PTK and installing the new one. Not tested on our side; worth a look when the 339bbc19 port is done, as a one-line
rule: do not clear the station's PN check on key removal, only on the next set.

## Arxusion/AirportMtkWlan v2.3.1-awdl-beta1 (notable, Medium)

[Release](https://github.com/Arxusion/AirportMtkWlan/releases/tag/v2.3.1-awdl-beta1) (GPL-3.0, so ideas only): AWDL on
MT7921 and MT7922 (Connac2) through the native `IO80211AWDLProtocol` (all nine vtable slots checked live instead of a
build gate), hardware OMAC (`HW_BSSID_1`), a standalone channel 6 AWDL TX and action frame RX, a kernel PF_ROUTE
fe80::%awdl0/64 prefix and permanent NDP neighbour routes for active peers, and an adaptive unicast rate ladder
(6 Mbps OFDM up to HT MCS7). It is the first public Tahoe AWDL path on a MediaTek card that is labelled working for
transfers, but still a beta with boot hangs reported on MT7921 ([issue 24](https://github.com/Arxusion/AirportMtkWlan/issues/24)).
For us: our AWDL/AirDrop item has nothing yet; the live-vtable approach and the NDP peer routes are the parts worth
reading again when we start. Connac2 against our Connac3 (MT7925) means the TX and OMAC parts do not carry over.
