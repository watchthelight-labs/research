# mt7925-wifi: 2026-10-30 (full run, window 2026-10-29 10:04 UTC to 2026-10-30)

## Notable

**[Borders-Q/MT7925-macOS26-package](https://github.com/Borders-Q/MT7925-macOS26-package)** repackages MtkWlan,
MtkWlanNative, MtkBt and LegionBtFix 0.3.0 for macOS 26 (25G241 and 25G332). The kernel binaries are byte for byte our
releases, pinned by SHA in `dependencies.lock.json`, with the matching source in `third_party/sources/` and credit in
`NOTICE.md`. Only the MtkWlan Info.plist is edited. Its README says it is untested on its target laptop (Alienware
AC16251), so reports from its users may reach our issue trackers.
