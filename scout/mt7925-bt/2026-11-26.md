# mt7925-bt: 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-26

Read from GitHub. Nothing below is reproduced on our machine.

Our open problem here is I-129: after a warm restart the MT7925 Bluetooth controller (`0489:E111`) does not enumerate on USB until the machine is powered off cold. We plan a shutdown quiesce.

- **Another MT7925 loader has not seen the USB device go missing. Its authors do not send a shutdown command.** The `MtkBtFw` loader in [thanhnndev/mt7925-macos](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/kexts/MtkBtFw/MtkBtFw.cpp) (device `0489:E11E`, an AMD ASUS laptop) registers no shutdown or restart handler and never sends WMT function control 0. On the warm restarts they document (12 October, 3 and 8 November) their [Bluetooth notes](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/docs/BLUETOOTH.md) record a hung firmware (watchdog fired, HCI silent) and recovery by a subsystem reset. They do not record a device that is absent from USB. Our loader sends function control 0 at restart. So a cheap test for I-129 is a build that skips that command at restart and relies on the start-up probe and reset. Different machine and chip variant, so this is a lead, not a cause. Confidence: Low to Medium. A second AMD ThinkBook with our loader also reports no missing controller on warm restarts ([user comment](https://github.com/Yangcheng811/ThinkBook-16p-2025-G6-ADR-Hackintosh/issues/1#issuecomment-7074407957), no logs).
- **Their 0.3.0 hold keeps bluetoothd from giving up on USB.** `bluetoothd` waits for the I/O Registry to go quiet, then scans for USB controllers once. If the firmware download is still running and the interface is open, it finds none, falls back to the UART placeholder, faults after 15 s and restarts. The authors saw this on 9 of 10 boots and fixed it by holding the service busy while the firmware loads, with a cap ([commit 4e4f9385](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/commit/4e4f9385), [0.3.1](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/commit/668f4003)). The price is a login window that comes 4 to 9 s later. Our loader has no such hold, and its two-pass path takes about 24 s ([report in WTLWireless #5](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLWireless/issues/5)). A user whose Bluetooth "cannot be turned on" although `MtkBtState` is ready may be hitting the same race. Worth a look when we see that symptom. Confidence: Medium (their machine, their logs). BSD-3-Clause-Clear licence, so reuse with attribution is possible.
- **Their 0.4.0 branch (device-level load, strict warm-restart HCI probe) was parked and never run** ([note](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/commit/5c2ab0f3)). Nothing to learn yet.
- **Arxusion's v2.2.3 warm boot fix is for Wi-Fi, not for Bluetooth.** It forces a firmware-own then driver-own cycle in the PCIe attach path of MT7921 and MT7922 cards when the host already owns the chip ([diff](https://github.com/Arxusion/AirportMtkWlan/compare/v2.2.2...v2.2.3)). The problem it addresses is a command timeout after a warm boot, not a missing USB device. Our Wi-Fi attach already does fw-own then drv-own every time. The same release moves `descriptor->complete()` of its Bluetooth hook until after the USB request, which fixed an early-boot hang on Tahoe ([issue 14](https://github.com/Arxusion/AirportMtkWlan/issues/14)). We do not hook USB requests, so it does not apply. GPL-3.0, ideas only. Confidence: High for what the diff does.
- **Linux fixed the same warm-restart class in August and needed an ACPI port reset.** The patch ([b3078bb2ee12](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/b3078bb2ee127900c126491ed4c9ddcd1ead76b7)) says the MT7925 Bluetooth function can enumerate after a warm reboot while the WMT command stays silent, and that recovery went through a USB reset that escalated to the ACPI `_PRR` port reset. This is older than the window. It supports the view that the missing-device case may need a port power reset that macOS never issues. Confidence: Medium.

Nothing new from the other repos in range: [Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26](https://github.com/Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26) only moved its README to WTLWireless 0.15.0 and reports no `bluetoothd` crash since 0.2.4.

Next on our side: build the I-129 test without function control 0 at restart, and note whether the controller is also missing in Windows after the same warm restart.
