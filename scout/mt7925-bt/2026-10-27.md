## Second run (Mac, window 2026-10-27 09:29 to 18:11 UTC)


### Actionable

**flxxxxddd/SimpleMtkBtFw#2: BLE HID pairing stalls on Tahoe for MT7922 and RZ616.**
[The issue](https://github.com/flxxxxddd/SimpleMtkBtFw/issues/2) (23 Oct) says bluetoothd waits for the LE PHY Update
Complete and LE Data Length Change events, and the MediaTek controller never sends them. The reporter's workaround hooks
`IOUSBHostIOSource::io` to inject synthetic events behind a `-smbtlefix` boot-arg; it is in an open PR (#3). The repo is
GPL-3.0, so we read it as ideas only, and it lists MT7921, RZ608 and MT7922 only. A cheap check on our side: pair a BLE
HID device (mouse or keyboard) through MtkBt and LegionBtFix on the MT7925 and see whether it stalls the same way.
Medium confidence.

### Notable
