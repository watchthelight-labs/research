# MediaTek MT7925 Bluetooth: what moved, 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-22

Target: [mt7925-hackintosh-bluetooth](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/mt7925-hackintosh-bluetooth) (MtkBt) and [LegionBtFix](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/LegionBtFix). Open: [#1](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/mt7925-hackintosh-bluetooth/issues/1), the warm-restart FUNC_CTRL report (0.2.3, then 0.2.4 with at most 2 resets per boot; waiting on the reporter). Confidence uses High / Medium / Low / Unverified.

## Actionable

**An HP MT7925 card stuck on the placeholder controller looks like our NVRAM key case** (Medium). [thanhnndev/ga403wm-hackintosh#10](https://github.com/thanhnndev/ga403wm-hackintosh/issues/10): an HP OEM card (USB 0e8d:8c38) on macOS 15.8.1. The firmware loads to `bt-state=ready`, but `system_profiler` shows `BCM_4350C2`, `Transport: UART`, `Address: NULL`. That's what bluetoothd falls back to when `bluetoothInternalControllerInfo` isn't exactly 14 bytes, which we traced on an ASRock X870 board. Unconfirmed until the reporter posts his NVRAM. It isn't UTBMap (the device enumerates fine) or LegionBtFix (he's on Sequoia, and LegionBtFix is gated to 25G241). His note that section 2 returns status 255 after a warm reboot is our #1 failure mode. MtkBt matches 0e8d:8c38 without plist edits.

Not answered from here; a reply is drafted in the run report.

## Notable

- **Two more machines use MtkBt** (High). [BelikPePe-UA/legion5-15irx10-hackintosh](https://github.com/BelikPePe-UA/legion5-15irx10-hackintosh) (Legion 5 15IRX10, Sequoia) and [Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26](https://github.com/Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26) (HP OMEN 16, Tahoe) run MtkBt 0.2.2 with the 14-byte key. Both should move to 0.2.4 for warm restarts.
- **MT7922 Bluetooth in ga403wm** (Medium). [ga403wm-hackintosh#7](https://github.com/thanhnndev/ga403wm-hackintosh/issues/7) adds MT7922 (0489:e0f6) firmware selection to MtkBtFw. A mouse works; AirPods connect but play about one time in fifteen. That looks like the A2DP and LE problems LegionBtFix solves on 25G241, but it's on Sequoia, so Unverified.

## Third run (afternoon)

- [ga403wm-hackintosh #10](https://github.com/thanhnndev/ga403wm-hackintosh/issues/10#issuecomment-6344939053): an HP MT7925 (`0e8d:8c38`) reaches bt-state=ready but macOS keeps the BCM_4350C2 UART placeholder. That is the symptom we had when `bluetoothInternalControllerInfo` was not exactly 14 bytes; we answered with the two BlueToolFixup NVRAM keys and the FUNC_CTRL 0 at shutdown for their warm-reboot status 255. Medium.
- For our I-084: a Legion Pro 5 with Intel Bluetooth also writes a shutdown_stall report after every shutdown with no third-party driver in the path ([power-sleep.md](https://github.com/Ammarrrrrrr/legion-pro5-hackintosh/blob/main/docs/power-sleep.md)). That weakens our guess that MtkBt or LegionBtFix causes ours. Low.
