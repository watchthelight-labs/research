# mt7925-bt: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-21

Read from GitHub and a short web search. Nothing below is reproduced on our machine.

Our open problem here is I-129: after a warm restart the MT7925 Bluetooth controller (`0489:E111`) does not enumerate on USB until the machine is powered off cold.

- **A Linux MT7927 package documents the same fault and blames standby power.** Its README says the Bluetooth USB device can vanish and stay gone across reboots because the controller keeps standby power, and that the BIOS ErP setting or pulling the power clears it ([jetm/mediatek-mt7927-dkms](https://github.com/jetm/mediatek-mt7927-dkms)). Linux desktop boards, not our laptop. Confidence: Medium.
- **The MT7927 repo built on our kexts separates two cases.** One is Bluetooth and some USB ports dead together, also in Windows, which it treats as a controller power state that needs a full power drain. The other is a half-loaded chip, which it says a reset before the firmware download fixed across warm reboots and a macOS, Windows, macOS cycle ([qqwqtzd/mt7927-hackintosh](https://github.com/qqwqtzd/mt7927-hackintosh)). Their machine, their report. It gives us a test to tell the two apart: does the controller also stay missing in Windows after the warm restart. Confidence: Medium.
- **Linux forum threads describe `0489:e111` missing after a reboot from Windows** ([Arch](https://bbs.archlinux.org/viewtopic.php?id=308168), [Ubuntu](https://discourse.ubuntu.com/t/bluetooth-issue-with-6-14-kernel/67171)). Search results only, not read in full. Confidence: Low.
- **AirportMtkWlan's Bluetooth patcher fixed a panic on Sequoia and later** by preparing the USB memory descriptor before reading from it ([v2.2.2](https://github.com/Arxusion/AirportMtkWlan/releases/tag/v2.2.2)). It is for MT7921 and MT7922 USB controllers. Worth a look at our own descriptor handling. GPL-3.0, ideas only.

Taken together these point at a hardware or firmware state, not at macOS. Next on our side: check whether the fault also shows in Windows after a warm restart, and whether the firmware setup has an ErP or always-on USB option.
