## Notable

**[CENSEDAXIS20025/ASUS-FX608LP-Hackintosh-Multiboot](https://github.com/CENSEDAXIS20025/ASUS-FX608LP-Hackintosh-Multiboot)**
(275HX and RTX 5070, an ELAN touchpad on I2C2) wrote up three traps on Arrow Lake-HX: VoodooI2C 2.9.1 returns early
from `getDeviceResources()` when the APIC pin looks valid, so `-vi2c-force-polling` is ignored; a controller class
name that stock VoodooI2C does not have; and VoodooI2CHID creating an interrupt source that never fires in polling
mode. They also ended up polling, as we did. No licence is stated.

**[bafcz/Hackintosh_LegionY9000P-IRX8_RaptorLake-HX 3c5a8f72](https://github.com/bafcz/Hackintosh_LegionY9000P-IRX8_RaptorLake-HX/commit/3c5a8f72)**
dropped its TPD0 GPIO SSDT and gets real GPIO interrupts on Raptor Lake through an INTC1085 VoodooGPIO fork. The same
commit lifts a 4.2 GHz cap with a Lilu plugin that rewrites XCPM's HWP requests. That is the same cause our
LegionCpuPower handles with per-core HWP ranges, solved another way. GPL-3.0, ideas only.
