# Arrow Lake I2C touchpads: what moved, 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-22

Target: [WTLI2CArrowLake](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLI2CArrowLake). Open: [#1](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLI2CArrowLake/issues/1) (outside report, Raptor Lake), [#2](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLI2CArrowLake/issues/2) polling at about 73 Hz, [#3](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLI2CArrowLake/issues/3) no input after a warm restart from Windows. Confidence uses High / Medium / Low / Unverified.

## Notable

- **The plain controller class works on another Arrow Lake board** (High). [iivn1026/ASUSTXGaming6ProSequoiaEFI](https://github.com/iivn1026/ASUSTXGaming6ProSequoiaEFI) (Core Ultra 7 255HX) edits VoodooI2C's Info.plist to match 7F4F, with the same Linux LPSS timings we use. It runs on the plain `VoodooI2CPCIController` class. Our 1.1.0 needed `VoodooI2CPCILakeController` because our controller boots in D3hot, so that requirement looks board-specific. No licence; it doesn't use our kext.
- **A 2025 Legion Y9000P EFI uses our device mask** (Medium). [wsjj-official/EFI-2025Y9000P](https://github.com/wsjj-official/EFI-2025Y9000P) matches VoodooI2C 2.9.1 with `0x7f4d8086&0xFFFCFFFF` on the plain class. Its boot-args repeat our NvGfx set, without credit. Speakers are Awinic AW88399 amplifiers on I2C2, not CS35L41. No licence.
- **NullMoth's installer bundles LegionI2CArrowLake 1.1.0** (High). [nullmoth/1401 bba6075de](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401/commit/bba6075de) adds it automatically, with VoodooI2C, when it finds matching hardware. It credits us under ISC.

## Third run (afternoon)

- New lead: [victorwitkamp/VoodooGPIO](https://github.com/victorwitkamp/VoodooGPIO) (GPL-3.0) makes the GPI_IS offset per-community and adds an Alder Lake-S personality; a Legion Pro 5 runs its touchpad in GPIO interrupt mode with it. No Arrow Lake personality yet, so it is a template for leaving polling mode, not a fix. Needs an audit before any build. Low.
