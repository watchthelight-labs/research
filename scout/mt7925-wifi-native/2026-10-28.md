## Notable

**New project: DrogaBox/RTL8814Native** ([repo](https://github.com/DrogaBox/RTL8814Native), created 2026-10-27): a
native IO80211 and Skywalk front end for the USB RTL8814AU, tested on 26.7.1 with one adapter. It dumps IO80211's
vtables per build (`Docs/re/io80211_25.6.0/`), generates a header from them and gates the build on it, and says to
re-dump after every macOS update because the slots move. That is the same per-build pin our MtkWlanNative uses; its
dump is an independent check for ours when we re-dump for 25G332. GPL-2.0, Realtek HAL from morrownr/8814au. High
confidence.

**AirPort-RTW** ([PR #5](https://github.com/JoMei9019-real/AirPort-RTW/pull/5)): WPA3-Personal and a faster software
CCMP on the legacy IO80211 front end. Does not touch the native ABI question. High confidence.
