# Native AirPort front ends on Tahoe: what moved, 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-22

Target: [mt7925-hackintosh-wifi-native](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/mt7925-hackintosh-wifi-native), a native IO80211 front end on 25G241. Open issues: [#1](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/mt7925-hackintosh-wifi-native/issues/1) channel width always 20 MHz, [#2](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/mt7925-hackintosh-wifi-native/issues/2) no AWDL. Confidence uses High / Medium / Low / Unverified.

Nothing new solves either open issue. Every "native AirPort on Tahoe" project found this week traces back to laobamac's itlwm at [738d4c6](https://github.com/laobamac/itlwm/tree/738d4c61b5a64433b27c1ab09dba94916ab77d8d/AirportItlwm), which we already credit. It also has no AWDL and also reports 20 MHz in scan results.

## Actionable

**qwz-macos arrived at our design independently** (High, code read). [f0xeri/qwz-macos](https://github.com/f0xeri/qwz-macos) (GPL-2.0, ideas only) runs OpenBSD qwz(4) for a Qualcomm WCN7850 in one kext and a native front end in another, with a C ops table between the two, a build allowlist of 25G241 only, and a HeliPort fallback, as ours has. Tested on one desktop on 26.7.1.
- **Current-network channel width** ([#1](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/mt7925-hackintosh-wifi-native/issues/1), current-network half): `getCHANNEL` in [QwzAirportInterface.cpp](https://github.com/f0xeri/qwz-macos/blob/main/airport/QwzAirportInterface.cpp) with [CurrentLink.hpp](https://github.com/f0xeri/qwz-macos/blob/main/airport/CurrentLink.hpp) reports 20/40/80/160 MHz and extension-above through `APPLE80211_C_FLAG_*`, which come from Apple's headers. Its scan results still use a fixed 20 MHz chanspec.
- **Sleep and wake** (we have none yet): [PowerRecovery.hpp](https://github.com/f0xeri/qwz-macos/blob/main/airport/PowerRecovery.hpp) and [RadioLifecycle.hpp](https://github.com/f0xeri/qwz-macos/blob/main/airport/RadioLifecycle.hpp) give the 25G241 power ioctl layout. On wake they replay the user's power state, send `POWER_CHANGED`, and bracket a reset around the available and unavailable messages.

Credited in our README.

## Notable

- **[Youtubefan01/airportitlwmtahoe](https://github.com/Youtubefan01/airportitlwmtahoe)** repackages laobamac's itlwm (High): its 31 Tahoe glue files are identical to `738d4c6`. Its README names `IO80211ControllerV2::createVirtualInterface` / `attachVirtualInterface` and `IO80211VirtualInterface` as the unused Tahoe hooks for AWDL. That's a pointer for [#2](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/mt7925-hackintosh-wifi-native/issues/2); nobody has driven them yet.
- **[xnoah222/Realtek-AirPort-Family](https://github.com/xnoah222/Realtek-AirPort-Family) and [Airport_RTW88](https://github.com/xnoah222/Airport_RTW88)** take the opposite route (High). On 14.4 and later they inject Ventura's IOSkywalkFamily and the legacy IO80211 family, as OCLP does. That gives working AWDL code, but only on the legacy family. Custom licence, ideas only.

## Noise

A Sequoia-pinned Realtek port marked "offline only", and six EFIs that use stock AirportItlwm or OCLP's legacy family.
