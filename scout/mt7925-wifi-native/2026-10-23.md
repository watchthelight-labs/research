# MT7925 native Wi-Fi front end: what moved, 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-23

## qwz-macos 0.0.26: what Tahoe asks before it shows 11ax, MCS and streams

[f0xeri/qwz-macos v0.0.26](https://github.com/f0xeri/qwz-macos/releases/tag/v0.0.26) (GPL-2.0, ideas only for our ISC front end) made `wdutil` and the Wi-Fi menu show 802.11ax, MCS, streams and the guard interval on 25G241. The commits say exactly which requests did it:

- [24deccaf](https://github.com/f0xeri/qwz-macos/commit/24deccaf742545208433ea3ed235ac32e89bab75): `getMCS_VHT` (slot 482, ioctl 0xc3), a packed `{version, mcs, nss, bw in MHz, gi 400 or 800}`. Once VHT or HE elements are in the current network's IEs, macOS asks this one and fails MCS, NSS and GI when it is unsupported.
- [626432da](https://github.com/f0xeri/qwz-macos/commit/626432dae17ce4ffc3784b481c18b2fadfd02d27): `getMCS` (479, 0x39) and `getGUARD_INTERVAL` (478, 0x37).
- [1eca16a8](https://github.com/f0xeri/qwz-macos/commit/1eca16a80d6e070015faa280ad5dbfd8211ede24): `CARD_CAPABILITIES` byte 0x15 bit 7 when HE is on; their reading of the KC is that `WCLDeviceConfiguration::isHeSupported` checks it.
- [77a2912c](https://github.com/f0xeri/qwz-macos/commit/77a2912c3ee02c6138bd567b54f1f9210626aa7b): HT Operation, VHT and HE Capabilities in scan-result IEs; the network's PHY mode is derived from those elements, `getPHY_MODE` is not asked on Tahoe.

On our side ([src/MtkWlanNativeReq.cpp](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/mt7925-hackintosh-wifi-native/blob/main/src/MtkWlanNativeReq.cpp)): `getCHANNEL` always answers 20 MHz although the core's `mtk_link_info` already carries `bw`, `center` and `nss`; the MCS, NSS and GI slots are stubs; and `commandAllowed()` refuses 0x37, 0x39, 0xc3 and 0x161 before any slot runs. That is the fix list for [#1](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/mt7925-hackintosh-wifi-native/issues/1), except the scan-list width, which qwz also still reports as 20 MHz (`Scan.hpp:70`). Effect High, KC reasoning Medium.

## AWDL on 25G241: thanhnndev's step-by-step

thanhnndev's [awdl-tahoe](https://github.com/thanhnndev/ga403wm-hackintosh/tree/awdl-tahoe) branch (Clear BSD, their clean-room `itlmt7925Native`, same chip and build as ours, not a fork of our kexts) is the most concrete AWDL work we have seen for a native Tahoe driver. From [docs/ROADMAP-AWDL.md](https://github.com/thanhnndev/ga403wm-hackintosh/blob/awdl-tahoe/docs/ROADMAP-AWDL.md) sections 9 and 10, with the panics and traces that back each step:

- wifip2pd creates `awdl0` itself with `VIRTUAL_IF_CREATE` (ioctl 0x5e, role 6) once the controller's command gate lets the AWDL ioctl range through. The virtual interface must subclass `IO80211AWDLProtocol` with BSD prefix `awdl`, created in the order of section 9.5; the Family then builds `IO80211AWDLPeerManager` itself.
- `configure()` aborts at the first unsupported setter (slot 540, `setAWDL_BSSID`, comes first); the channel sequence, master channel and sync state are echoed back.
- The Family never sends action frames. The driver builds PSF and MIF frames from the sync-frame TLV and sends them at 6 Mb/s inside a ROC window on channels 6, 44 and 149. Receive needs the firmware sniffer, and frames go to `actionFrameInput` (slot 73) in `IO80211DynamicBufferPool` buffers, the "dynamic-pool AWDL RX fix" of [7759e803](https://github.com/thanhnndev/ga403wm-hackintosh/commit/7759e803) merged in [c98bcff8](https://github.com/thanhnndev/ga403wm-hackintosh/commit/c98bcff84ae1eec728356e1077cdf53f1cc49f1f): a static pool panics in `flushPool` and a malloc pool's buffers lack `writeBytes`.
- Where it stands: real peer frames reach the peer manager, `peers:0`, AirDrop not working; the 2026-10-23 boot outcome is not recorded. An iPhone only starts AWDL after a BLE advertisement, so two Macs are the cleaner test.

Code High, their KC offsets Medium, outcome Unverified. The code is reusable with attribution, which our NOTICE already carries for their 24H32 work.

[kgp-macPro/AirportItlwm-Tahoe](https://github.com/kgp-macPro/AirportItlwm-Tahoe) (GPL-2.0, 21 stars) is not what its description suggests: it is upstream itlwm built for Ventura's legacy `IO80211FamilyLegacy` under OCLP root patches, and its only AWDL change removes the Ventura early return in `setVIRTUAL_IF_CREATE` and attaches a legacy `IO80211P2PInterface` named awdl. No action frames, no sync, no AirDrop claim. Nothing in it applies to a Tahoe-native driver. High.

Two more AWDL attempts appeared in the window, neither shown working: [Dino-VN/SimpleMtkWlan](https://github.com/Dino-VN/SimpleMtkWlan/tree/awdl) (an `awdl` branch on a MediaTek Skywalk front end for Tahoe) and [Youtubefan01/airportitlwmtahoe](https://github.com/Youtubefan01/airportitlwmtahoe) (an `IO80211AWDLProtocol` class for AX210, measured against 26.5, never loaded; its note that a virtual interface which does not redeclare the framework's overridden slots gets the base `createPeerManager` is worth keeping). Their slot numbers differ from thanhnndev's by 2 to 3, so re-check before reuse.
