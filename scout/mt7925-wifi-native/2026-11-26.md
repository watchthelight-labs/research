# mt7925-wifi-native: 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-26

Read from GitHub. Nothing below is reproduced on our machine. We are at AWDL phase 0: no `awdl0`, no virtual interface.

- **Another MT7925 macOS Wi-Fi project now ships a native front end with AWDL work, and it is not built on ours.** [thanhnndev/mt7925-macos](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos) released [v2.0-beta.1](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/releases/tag/v2.0-beta.1) (native IO80211/Skywalk, AWDL behind a boot argument) and [bt-v1.1](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/releases/tag/bt-v1.1). The kexts are `itlmt7925`, `itlmt7925Native` and `MtkBtFw`, bundle ids `com.itlmt7925.*`. A search of every kext, script and test file for `watchthelight`, `WTLWireless`, `MtkWlan`, `MtkCoreApi` and `MtkNativeAbi` found nothing. Its [NOTICE](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/NOTICE.md) says the hardware layer is a re-implementation of Linux mt76 and the class layouts come from reading Apple's kernel collections. So no credit is owed to us. The code is Clear BSD (BSD-3-Clause-Clear). Its release notes call it vibe-coded and tested on one laptop. Confidence: High for the provenance, High that it runs only on its author's machine so far.
- **It has what we lack: an `awdl0` interface and a first Mac to Mac AirDrop.** Its notes record `awdl0` created by the stock `wifip2pd`, TCP over AWDL on 4 November, one completed Mac to Mac AirDrop on 11 November and an AirDrop to a nearby MacBook on 23 November. The 25 November work is still open: nearby devices follow another AWDL master and the trees do not merge ([ROADMAP-AWDL 10.93 to 10.95](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP-AWDL.md)). iPhone to Mac does not work yet. Test-only, one machine. Confidence: Medium.
- **The phase 1 recipe is in their notes, and a second project agrees on the first step.** Facts, each from disassembly they publish:
  - `wifip2pd` creates `awdl0` only if the card capability array has the AWDL bit. In our `getCARD_CAPABILITIES` that is byte `0x08`, bit 0, which we leave clear ([section 10.45](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP-AWDL.md)). DrogaBox's Realtek layer sets the same bit and leaves NAN clear ([RTLAirLayer.cpp](https://github.com/DrogaBox/RTL8814Native/blob/main/Source/AirLayer/RTLAirLayer.cpp)).
  - `wifip2pd` then sends `APPLE80211_IOC_VIRTUAL_IF_CREATE` (0x5e) with a role. Role 6 is AWDL. The driver builds a subclass of `IO80211VirtualInterface` (that returns `awdl` as BSD prefix), registers it with `registerEthernetInterface`, calls `setAwdlInterface` on the controller, and the Family creates the peer manager on its own ([section 9.1](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP-AWDL.md)).
  - The Family treats `awdl0` as idle after 2 s without link activity unless the driver reports it. They call `IO80211PeerManager::setLastUnicastRx/TxLinkActivity`; DrogaBox writes the same counters ([section 10.95](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP-AWDL.md), [DrogaBox notes](https://github.com/DrogaBox/RTL8814Native/blob/main/Docs/AWDL_MODO_DATOS.md)).
  - A peer counts for the election only if heard in the last 3 s, and expires after 6 s. Their flicker problem is this rule.
  - AirDrop also needs Bluetooth LE advertising to work; their bottleneck was `bluetoothd` ([section 10.41](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP-AWDL.md)).
  Useful for phase 1 and 2. Confidence: High that the notes say this, Medium that every offset holds on every build. Licence: Clear BSD, reuse with credit is possible.
- **Sleep: an advertised keepalive offload made macOS wait 17.5 s.** The Family published `mDNS_KEY` and `mDNS_Keepalive` on their interface, so `apsd` waited for sleep proxy records the card could not take. They stopped the interface storing the two keys ([section 10.48](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP-AWDL.md)). Our driver only acknowledges sleep, and I found no code of ours that filters those keys. Check `ioreg -l -w0 | grep mDNS_` on our interface and time a real sleep. Confidence: Medium (their machine, one test).
- **Regulatory country from the AP, and a sniffer width bug.** They derive the country from the AP's Country element and send it with the channel domain and power tables, because the world domain "00" let 5 GHz frames out only on the AP's channel ([section 10.67](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP-AWDL.md)). Our front end reads the AP's country but only reports it. This matters for off-channel work such as AWDL and for the CN versus US report in [Game-5040 issue 1](https://github.com/Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26/issues/1). Their second fix: the sniffer command takes its own width codes (20 and 40 MHz 0, 80 MHz 1, 160 MHz 2), and passing the other table made the firmware stop answering on a 160 MHz AP ([section 10.84](https://github.com/thanhnndev/mt7925-macos/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP-AWDL.md)). We do not use the sniffer yet. Confidence: Medium.
- **No CSA, roaming, MLO, WPA3, 6 GHz or sleep handling there either.** Their roadmap lists sleep and wake, WPA3 and country handling as open. Nothing to borrow for I-139 or I-140.
- **Other AWDL work, read for method only.** [DrogaBox/RTL8814Native](https://github.com/DrogaBox/RTL8814Native) (Realtek, GPL-2.0, docs partly MIT) has a staged spec for role 6, skips `llw0` in stage 1 because the daemon tolerates its failure, and reads the sync template and channel sequence from the kernel before building its own ([notes](https://github.com/DrogaBox/RTL8814Native/blob/main/Docs/AWDL_LLW_COMPANION.md)). [ForbiddenItlwm](https://github.com/retropineaple/ForbiddenItlwm) 0.7.0 alphas (Intel, GPL-2.0) audit all 568 vtable slots of the AWDL interface and stay inert on a mismatch ([how it works](https://github.com/retropineaple/ForbiddenItlwm/blob/main/docs/HOW-IT-WORKS.md)). It has open reports that a BLE alias expiry stops AirDrop receivers ([issue 102](https://github.com/retropineaple/ForbiddenItlwm/issues/102)) and that Handoff advertising holds a sleep assertion ([issue 111](https://github.com/retropineaple/ForbiddenItlwm/issues/111), unverified). [AirPort-RTW](https://github.com/JoMei9019-real/AirPort-RTW) targets the older Ventura interface (role 4), so it does not apply. [qiqco/MT7922-macOS-Driver](https://github.com/qiqco/MT7922-macOS-Driver) claims "stable AirDrop" but ships kext zips only, with no source and no licence. Nothing there can be checked. All of these are GPL or unlicensed: ideas only.

AMD users of Arxusion's AirportMtkWlan report boot freezes when AWDL is enabled even in 2.2.3 ([issue 24](https://github.com/Arxusion/AirportMtkWlan/issues/24)), against the release note that says it is now safe. Keep AWDL behind a boot argument on our side too.

Next on our side: read sections 9 and 10.45 of their roadmap against our 25G332 dump, then set the AWDL capability bit and answer role 6 in a build that stays off unless a boot argument is set.
