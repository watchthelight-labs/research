  MT7921 and MT7922. They are now a checklist in our MT7921 backend plan. Write-up:
  [mt7925-wifi](../mt7925-wifi/2026-11-13.md).

  the link-quality snapshot with ours.

  and Sequoia. GPL-2.0, so ideas only, but its description of the macOS side is input for our AWDL work. Write-up:
  [mt7925-wifi-native](../mt7925-wifi-native/2026-11-13.md).
