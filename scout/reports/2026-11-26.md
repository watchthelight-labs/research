# Upstream scout run, 2026-11-26

Targets: all eleven. Window: 2026-11-21 23:51 UTC to 2026-11-26. Full run with pull requests. The web sweep was
not done for the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth targets. 45 known repositories had changes and 40 new candidates turned up; 12
related repositories were added to the watchlist. Read from GitHub only; nothing here is reproduced on our machine,
and our test laptop is limited to short boots at the moment.

## Actionable

- NullMoth 3.1 fixes a boot guard fault that 2.9 and 3.0 have: a boot right after a failed start silently comes up
  without NVAccel and NVRMFB ([commit d06c1bef](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/commit/d06c1bef),
  issue 633). Our fork is on 2.9. Next: carry the latch over, and read the guard counter before a test boot.
  Write-up: [nvgfx](../nvgfx/2026-11-26.md).
- LenovoLegionLinux v0.1.9 documents a fan-level write through WMI that was validated on a sibling model with our
  BIOS (S2CN26WW): both fans went from 2300 to 3000 RPM and back. One unit, Linux, on AC, custom mode, raising only.
  Our note that no safe fan write is known for this firmware is out of date as worded. Next: a read of the level
  table, nothing more. Write-up: [legion-efi](../legion-efi/2026-11-26.md).
- thanhnndev's MT7925 driver now has an `awdl0` interface and a first Mac to Mac AirDrop by its notes. Two facts we
  can check in our own front end: the AWDL bit in the card capability array, and link activity reporting to the peer
  manager. Its Bluetooth loader sends no shutdown command, which gives a test for our missing-controller fault
  (I-129): skip ours at restart once. Write-ups: [mt7925-wifi-native](../mt7925-wifi-native/2026-11-26.md),
  [mt7925-bt](../mt7925-bt/2026-11-26.md).
- Linux mt76 has three fixes for gaps our driver shares: the 5.9 GHz channels in the per-rate power list
  ([99265f9a](https://github.com/openwrt/mt76/commit/99265f9a)), reading the firmware's status after a command
  ([bc394ec4](https://github.com/openwrt/mt76/commit/bc394ec4)) and the group key packet number per TID
  ([339bbc19](https://github.com/openwrt/mt76/commit/339bbc19)). Write-up: [mt7925-wifi](../mt7925-wifi/2026-11-26.md).
- A third Arrow Lake-H laptop reports an accelerated desktop on our 0.0.48 kernel driver, with a change to the Metal
  plugin's `textureBarrier` that its owner says cures blur and ghosting. Design only for us until it is built and
  tested. Its boot framebuffer address is the fourth one our hardware cursor check refuses, so the check has to take
  the address the firmware left instead of one constant. Write-up: [xe](../xe/2026-11-26.md).
- A Panther Lake tester published his sleepable SRCU as a separate repository
  ([lems-hardware/lhl-srcu-patch](https://github.com/lems-hardware/lhl-srcu-patch), BSD-2-Clause). Same shape as the
  fix sketched for our shim, plus an epoch re-check ours lacked.
- A Legion 5 Pro 2025 EFI ships six of our builds with no mention of the source. A credit request is open
  ([issue](https://github.com/KSLengYu/Legion5Pro-2025-Hackintosh/issues/1)).

## Notable

- NullMoth 3.1 has nothing new for hybrid laptops: `nmhybrid` is still untested upstream, head 4 gets no frames on
  two testers, and the 3.0 package shipped the older NMIntelFB. The 2.8.1 release of 2026-11-25 is a back-filled tag
  with the 2.8 driver.
- NVIDIA's 620.70.10 tag is a datacenter branch point, not a successor of 620.73.04.
- HighDelay's NVK fork reports hardware VP9 decode in Safari on an RTX 2060 SUPER; laptop panels are untested there.
- A Dell G15 5530 on NullMoth 3.1 powers its RTX off at idle through an ACPI method or the GC6 boot-args.
- An HP OMEN 16 write-up keeps panel brightness across boot and wake with a daemon and a watcher.
- Two projects boot Sound Open Firmware from a macOS kext for the internal microphone
  ([VoodooHDA-SOF](https://github.com/new-green/VoodooHDA-SOF), LatSOF-Audio). Write-up: [audio](../audio/2026-11-26.md).
- VoodooI2C pull request 595 sets the GPIO interrupt type before asking the controller, a shorter fix than the one
  we drafted. Write-up: [i2c](../i2c/2026-11-26.md).
- A native RGB tool for Legion 4-zone keyboards talks to the ITE USB controller directly; our controller is not in
  its table. Write-up: [ite-kbd](../ite-kbd/2026-11-26.md).
- AirportMtkWlan 2.2.3 fixes a warm boot fault with an own-cycle at attach, which our driver already does.
