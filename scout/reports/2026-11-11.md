## Actionable

- [NullMoth v2.9](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/releases/tag/v2.9) (2026-11-10): the last 2.x, 529
  commits after 2.8.1. It rewrites the shader cache write and adds a shader warm-up after login, which is the code our
  slow-drag fix touches. Next: rebase onto 2.9 instead of 2.8.1 and measure stock 2.9 first. Write-up:
  [nvgfx](../nvgfx/2026-11-11.md).
- Two of our state-cache commits are in NullMoth since 2.7.1, with switches to turn them off. The Alacritty test we
  promised on [#412](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/412) is still owed.
- NullMoth 2.9 has its own hybrid laptop mode (`nmhybrid`), not yet run on a hybrid laptop by its author. The first
  report is a boot panic on Alder Lake ([#555](https://github.com/nullmoth/nvidia-macos-driver/issues/555)).
- [ArrowWhatever](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever) patches 0010 to 0037 on our 7D67: a hardware cursor,
  scaled sizes through the plane scaler, brightness through i915, AGDC colour stages and a GPU clock floor. Next: read
  0023, 0025, 0030 and 0035 against our tree. Write-up: [xe](../xe/2026-11-11.md).
- Scaled HiDPI on an i915 panel is solved on [zep245's driver](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver); the
  recipe is in the xe write-up.
- [hepengfei00/XeMac](https://github.com/hepengfei00/XeMac): our tree on a second 7D67 machine with an external
  DisplayPort screen and nine patches of its own. Next: read 0038 to 0046.
- FreeBSD LinuxKPI fixes for hangs to check against our shim, listed in the xe write-up.
- HE association and an IPv6 fix documented by the MT7927 port; a link-quality report that Tahoe expects every few
  seconds. Write-up: [mt7925-wifi](../mt7925-wifi/2026-11-11.md).
- Bluetooth after a warm restart: two projects found the firmware still running and handle it. Write-up:
  [mt7925-bt](../mt7925-bt/2026-11-11.md).
