# Legion audio (VoodooHDA-Legion): what moved, 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-22

Target: [WTLAudio](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLAudio), VoodooHDA pinned at `beb415a` with a Legion patch, Realtek ALC257 on Tahoe. No open issues. Confidence uses High / Medium / Low / Unverified.

## Actionable

**Don't move past `beb415a` without restoring one line** (High, diff read). [CloverHackyColor/VoodooHDA b320ac936](https://github.com/CloverHackyColor/VoodooHDA/commit/b320ac936) "repair AMD HDMI audio" comments out `if (res == -1) res = 0;` in `Parser.cpp` (`switchHandler` / `switchInit`) for every codec, not only AMD. A failed pin-sense verb now reads as "jack present". On our ALC257 a single verb timeout could move output to the headphones and mute the speakers. If we bump the pin, put the line back in `legion.patch`. BSD-2-Clause.

## Notable

- **Intel HDMI presence polling** (Medium). [4545ddc04](https://github.com/CloverHackyColor/VoodooHDA/commit/4545ddc04) adds a 10 s presence poll for Intel display codecs, plus D0 power-up and pin enable around each stream. It only helps once the Intel iGPU drives a display, and ours (7D67) has no macOS framebuffer, so we don't need it now. Our Info.plist leaves out HDMI on purpose.
- **USB microphones and the BL build** (High). [VoodooHDA#29](https://github.com/CloverHackyColor/VoodooHDA/issues/29) shows why the OpenCore-injected build breaks USB audio: it carries its own IOAudioFamily, so Apple's fails on duplicate class names and AppleUSBAudio never loads. Our `/Library/Extensions` build avoids that.

## Noise

[VoodooHDA#31](https://github.com/CloverHackyColor/VoodooHDA/issues/31) (ALC230, VoodooHDA 3.6.8) is a different codec and version. Seven discovery hits (VM tools, AppleALC guides for a Tahoe without AppleHDA, EFIs for other laptops) don't apply.
