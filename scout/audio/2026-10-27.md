## Notable

**AW88399 speaker amps on the Legion Y9000P IAX10H.** The
[wsjj-official 1.0 EFI](https://github.com/wsjj-official/EFI-2025Y9000P/commit/ebc42e5e10) for the 83F5 drives both
Awinic AW88399 amps (I2C 0x34 and 0x35, ACPI `AWDZ8399` at `\_SB.PC02.I2C2.SPKR`) with a kext, plus a shared-DAC fork of
AppleALC because pins 17 and 14 both sit on DAC 0x02 (without it AppleHDA programs four channels and the sound is quiet,
high and stuttering). It also fixes a `layout-id` written as an 8-byte string. The kext comes from
[Ersiter/Legion-ALC287-AW88399-macOS](https://github.com/Ersiter/Legion-ALC287-AW88399-macOS) (GPL-2.0), a port of the
Linux `snd_hda_scodec_aw88399` core; the AppleALC fork is [Ersiter/AppleALC](https://github.com/Ersiter/AppleALC). The
amp firmware comes from Lenovo's Windows package. Speakers work on the owner's 83F5 under Sequoia 15.8; headphones,
HDMI audio and sleep are untested. Our 83F0 reports an ALC257 codec and its speakers already work, and we have not seen
an AW88399 node in its tables, so this probably does not apply to us. Worth a check of our DSDT before closing it.
