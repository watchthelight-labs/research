## Notable

**LenovoLegionLinux: Gen 10 EC offsets measured on a Legion 5 15IRX10.**
[PR #642](https://github.com/johnfanv2/LenovoLegionLinux/pull/642) corrects the EC 0x5508 layout used by Gen 10
machines that only expose WMI: fan speeds at 0xC406 and 0xC403 (in units of 100 RPM), CPU temperature at 0xC4B0, GPU
temperature at 0xC4B4, power mode in bits 0 to 3 of 0xC420. The custom fan table has ten levels at 0xF9F0 to 0xF9F9 and
only applies in custom mode (level 10 gives 4600 RPM, level 1 gives 1800). Tested on 83LY, not our 83F0, though a
comment in the thread says the layout matches the S2CN BIOS family ours belongs to. The fan curve issue for several
machines including 83F0 ([#491](https://github.com/johnfanv2/LenovoLegionLinux/issues/491)) is still open. High
confidence for 83LY, Low to Medium for 83F0 until we read our own EC.

### Notable

**Arrow Lake boot hangs at `EXITBS:START` have a documented BIOS cause on other machines.** Our I-118 (four macOS starts
that died after `EXITBS:START` with no macOS log, N-292) has no cause yet, and these are the closest cases:
- [nullmoth/1401#110](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401/issues/110) (26 Oct): a ThinkBook 16p 2025 with a Core Ultra 7
  255HX and an RTX 5060 Laptop. The `EXITBS:START` hang went away with BIOS VT-d off (the ACPI DMAR table disappears);
  a `Non-monotonic time` panic went away with `AppleXcpmExtraMsrs`; a BlueToolFixup crash with the Intel Bluetooth
  kexts off; the fourth stop is a page fault in `sync_tos_to_lks`, which the reporter ties to a MADT that lists 48
  processors for a 20 core CPU without SMT. Four stages, each with evidence.
- [ydd1126/skybreak](https://github.com/ydd1126/skybreak): an Arrow Lake-S 245K with an RTX 3070 on NullMoth through
  1401; its notes name BIOS VT-d off and `DevirtualiseMmio` true for the same hang. No raw logs, written with an AI
  agent playbook. Related; not credited anywhere yet.
- [1401#58](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401/issues/58) (Core Ultra 7 255H, closed after seven comments) found the same
  VT-d cause. [1401#64](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401/issues/64) (RTX 5070 Ti, panel wired to the dGPU) freezes
  right after `EXITBS:START` and is still open. [1401#115](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401/issues/115) (Alder Lake,
  VT-d already off) hangs at the same line, so VT-d is not the only cause.

For us: our Legion boots most of the time (N-293 passed) and our config already sets `DisableIoMapper`, so VT-d is
probably not the I-118 cause, but the BIOS VT-d and DMAR state at N-292 is worth recording. Medium confidence.

- [Mountainatic/YaoShi16-Ultra-OpenCore-efi](https://github.com/Mountainatic/YaoShi16-Ultra-OpenCore-efi) (MIT): the EFI
  of a MECHREVO Yaoshi 16 Ultra (275HX, RTX 5080 Laptop), NvGfx 2.5.3 boot-args, panel on NVIDIA.
- johnfanv2/LenovoLegionLinux has a baseline-support issue for the Legion 9i Gen 10 (83EY). Not our model.
