# legion-efi and audio: 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-21

Read from GitHub only. Nothing below is reproduced on our machine.

- **LenovoLegionLinux has no change for our model in the window, but what it already knows about it matters now.** Machine type 83F0 is matched by its BIOS prefix, and temperatures, fan speed and power mode go through Lenovo's WMI methods, not through raw reads of the embedded controller. Offsets borrowed from another model gave nonsense fan speeds on this chassis ([#359](https://github.com/johnfanv2/LenovoLegionLinux/issues/359)). The fan table is ten levels with no temperature fields, custom mode only accepts levels at or above the defaults, and writing percent values as levels powered off a different Legion a few seconds later ([#491](https://github.com/johnfanv2/LenovoLegionLinux/issues/491)). For us: read fans and temperatures through WMI, as we do from Windows, and treat any write to the fan table as unsafe until our own DSDT confirms the layout. GPL-2.0, ideas only. Confidence: Medium-High for the layout, Low for write safety on our unit.
- **A VoodooHDA 4.1 build with IOAudioFamily embedded is reported on Tahoe 26.7.1.** The report names two build fixes: `-fno-stack-protector`, and a guard around the two IOGraphicsFamily calls. It also notes that one of the bundled plists matches only the device name `HDAS` and not `HDEF` ([#32](https://github.com/CloverHackyColor/VoodooHDA/issues/32)). Self-reported on one older machine, no logs. Our audio build already embeds IOAudioFamily, so this is a list of three things to compare. Confidence: Low.
- **A Legion Y9000P 2025 EFI turned CpuTopologyRebuild on and dropped `TSC_sync_margin=0`** ([repo](https://github.com/wsjj-official/EFI-2025Y9000P)). No reasons given and no licence. Noted because both touch CPU bring-up on a close relative of our machine. Confidence: Low.

Web search for the Lenovo CBMR panic, the ITE keyboard under macOS and a 15IAX10 EFI found nothing new in the window.
