# Legion 5 15IAX10 EFI: what moved, 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-23

- No new release of OpenCorePkg, Lilu, VirtualSMC or RestrictEvents. OpenCorePkg master bumped the OSInfo protocol to revision 4 with a forced VT-d flag ([78d5ff82](https://github.com/acidanthera/OpenCorePkg/commit/78d5ff824992d5b227f842e34d8221eb8f31baf1)); nothing in our EFI uses it.
- [JungyoKim/Y540-17IRH-TAHOE](https://github.com/JungyoKim/Y540-17IRH-TAHOE): a Legion Y540 on Tahoe with a NullMoth 1.13 build that sleeps and wakes, patches public. The base is too old to apply to 2.x, but the list of what had to change is a checklist for our sleep work. Medium.
- [Game-5040](https://github.com/Game-5040/hackintosh-hp-8f06-macos26) fixed AC state on an HP by deriving `_PSR` from an EC bit the firmware does maintain, and found its FADT has no S3 at all. Two checks for us: which EC fields our battery methods read, and whether the 15IAX10's FADT declares S3.
- ECEnabler moved to [averycblack/ECEnabler](https://github.com/averycblack/ECEnabler); the watchlist follows the new owner.
- Nothing in the window on Thunderbolt or USB4 for Arrow Lake-HX.
