# Legion 5 15IAX10 OpenCore: what moved, 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-22

Target: [WTLLegionOpenCore](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLLegionOpenCore) (Arrow Lake HX, OpenCore 1.0.8, Tahoe 25G241). Open issues: [#1](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLLegionOpenCore/issues/1) battery readings, [#2](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLLegionOpenCore/issues/2) Thunderbolt/USB4 controller not found. Confidence uses High / Medium / Low / Unverified.

## Notable

- **Arrow Lake self-wake after sleep** (Medium to Low for laptops). [saspol/UltraWake](https://github.com/saspol/UltraWake) (MIT). On Arrow Lake-S with Z890, GPE 0x6B and 0x6E stay set because macOS never runs the firmware's `_L6B` / `_L6E` handlers, so every sleep after the first ends in a self-wake 10 to 20 s later. A `_PTS` SSDT clears the PCH and SoC status bits, and a kext turns a USB-keyboard DarkWake into a full wake. The README excludes laptops, but Arrow Lake HX is built from the S die with the same 800-series chipset (our HDA 7F50 and I2C 7F4x), and our ACPI path is `\_SB.PC02` like theirs, so it may apply to us. For later, when sleep is turned on. Credited.
- **Our fan interface is documented** (High). [johnfanv2/LenovoLegionLinux](https://github.com/johnfanv2/LenovoLegionLinux) (GPL-2.0, facts only) supports our model as `model_s2cn` (83F0/S2CN): EC chip id 0x5508, WMI3 for temperatures, fan speeds, power mode and the fan curve, a 10-level fan table at EC 0xF9F0 to 0xF9F9 with no temperature fields, and the EC path `\_SB.PC00.LPCB.EC0.VPC0`. Its EC3 offsets are wrong for this chip and unsafe to write. Their issue #491 (fan curve on EC 0x5508) is still open. Credited.
- **Arrow Lake HX EXITBS hang on a MUX laptop** (Low). [nullmoth/1401#64](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401/issues/64) (290HX, RTX 5070 Ti): reporters say turning VT-d off fixes it.
- **"Non-monotonic time" panic on a 2023 Legion** (Low). [nullmoth/1401#69](https://github.com/nullmoth/1401/issues/69), with AppleIntelCPUPowerManagement in the backtrace. Same Legion firmware family as ours.

## Noise

The OpenCorePkg commits in the window (APM media detection, an unused header removed, OpenCanopy button darkening, Eg2Info revision, a firmware-password refactor) are all after 1.0.8 and don't change our EFI, which uses the built-in picker. A CS35L41 amplifier kext needs AppleHDA, which Tahoe no longer has. A Chromebook port is unrelated.

## Third run (afternoon)

- [CpuTopologyRebuild #24](https://github.com/b00t0x/CpuTopologyRebuild/issues/24#issuecomment-6344938654): a Lenovo Core Ultra 5 125U stops at EXITBS:START with no kernel text. The APIC table in their attachment is 856 bytes with 48 x2APIC entries and no Local APIC entries, the same shape that stopped our 15IAX10 (XNU counts zero CPUs); their config had the replacement disabled. We pointed them at our [x2apic_to_lapic.py](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLLegionOpenCore/blob/main/scripts/x2apic_to_lapic.py). Medium-High that it is one of their blockers.
