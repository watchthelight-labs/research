# xe: 2026-10-24 (quick run, window 2026-10-23 23:28 to 2026-10-24 12:36 UTC)

## Notable

**kitsuneislife/nmx-intel: one-colour composites despite a finished GPU wait.** Their NullMoth 2.5.4 port, where the
RTX composites the desktop and NVAccel copies frames to an Intel panel, saw the panel blink black: some composites
reached the copy still a single colour although the GPU wait had reported them ready. Their patch
(`accel-0004-hold-blank-frames.patch`, [2910e8a](https://github.com/kitsuneislife/nmx-intel/commit/2910e8a6d9)) keeps
up to three one-colour frames in a row off the panel when it flips RAM buffers, and counts them
(`debug.nvaccel_iop_blank_held`). The [follow-up note](https://github.com/kitsuneislife/nmx-intel/commit/606a2c2118)
says the remaining blinks are not one-colour frames. This bears on our I-108 note that frames copied from the second
swap source in N-278 and N-280 hashed to 0. Theirs is NVIDIA compositing; ours is Intel through XeGfxAccel, so the
cause may differ. Medium relevance, Unverified for our stack. The repo has no licence, so we take the idea, not the code.

**WhateverXe 0.5.0 and WhateverXe-Graphics 0.2.0** ([0.5.0](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/releases/tag/0.5.0),
[v0.2.0](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe-Graphics/releases/tag/v0.2.0)): Alder Lake-S support, Tiger Lake GT1
resources, Display 12 register tracing, USB-C reconnection hangs documented as not OS-specific. Not Arrow Lake, so
nothing changes for us. High confidence on what changed.

**JulioNectar/Hackintosh-XPS13Plus-IrisXe-Tahoe**: "GPUREC v2" recovers render-engine hangs on Apple's own Intel driver
without a freeze ([1c792c4](https://github.com/JulioNectar/Hackintosh-XPS13Plus-IrisXe-Tahoe/commit/1c792c4b14)),
an 80 EU topology boot-arg, H.264 encode notes. A different stack from ours (Apple's driver on Tiger Lake), so ideas
only.
