## Actionable

**Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever: patches on our XeGfx for a MECHREVO Yaoshi 16 Ultra (275HX, 8086:7D67).**
[Repo](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever). It is built on NvGfx `xe` 5a19a73f (XeGfx 0.0.44) and credits
us. WindowServer composes on the Arrow Lake iGPU at about 60 fps (2560x1600). The panel is still lit by the firmware.
Four patches are worth a line-by-line comparison with our current tree:
- 0006: WindowServer hands the plugin read-only host memory, so they import only writable ranges and copy the rest
  (`nvmtl_range_writable`). Before that they saw EFAULT and DEVICE_LOST.
- 0001: a page hold/unwire race in userptr import.
- 0008 and 0009 (K9): a zero-copy flip through spare GGTT slots, with the plane address armed only after the GPU is
  idle. Their no-wait experiment broke the ordering between GPU writes and scanout. That is the same family as the
  band bug we fixed today (I-119), on the display side.
- 0018, 0020, 0021: EDID over AUX and an Intel-named framebuffer subclass, so CoreDisplay shows a real display name.

Their `docs/cursor.md` uses a software cursor only and says nothing about a square around the cursor. The patches are
PolyForm Noncommercial, the same licence as XeGfx. Confidence: High on the content, Medium on whether each one applies
to our current code.

**zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver: brightness that survives login.** Commits
[6dd5d05c](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver/commit/6dd5d05c),
[62e231d5](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver/commit/62e231d5),
[b3b1bb68](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver/commit/b3b1bb68) and
[da5bd8ab](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver/commit/da5bd8ab), all in `nmx_fb.cpp`. They keep the user's
level apart from the macOS dim factor, treat values of 3 or less as a dim request, ignore jumps of more than 10 points
above the user level, and re-sync the stored parameters a few times after boot because macOS restores its saved slider
value at login. No SPDX licence, so we take the idea only. High confidence on what it does.

## Notable

- zep245 [fda03b89](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver/commit/fda03b89) bounds the i915 commit fence wait
  during boot and logs fences that never signal. Worth keeping in mind for our freeze hunt (I-116); no link proven.
- zep245 [b44e0a40](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver/commit/b44e0a40) binds the address table with a
  dynamic offset, so draws that change only buffers reuse their descriptor set. A frame-cost idea for our ANV path.
- [steelbrain/metal2vulkan](https://github.com/steelbrain/metal2vulkan) merges a copied host buffer back at its
  completion turn (76d89d87, 3fd1f667, 44eaa098). That matches how we fixed the bands: the writer waits for its
  work before the reader sees the surface. The rest of the 43 commits are NVIDIA conformance work.
- [JulioNectar #20](https://github.com/JulioNectar/Hackintosh-XPS13Plus-IrisXe-Tahoe/issues/20): their Metal adapter
  replaced the JVM's SIGSEGV and SIGBUS handlers and IntelliJ crashed. A plugin loaded into every Metal app must not
  install signal handlers. We will check ours.
