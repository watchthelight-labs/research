# xe: 2026-10-30 to 2026-11-11

Read from GitHub only. Nothing below is reproduced on our machine unless it says so.

## ArrowWhatever: patches 0010 to 0037

[Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever) (Core Ultra 9 275HX, 7D67, the same chip as ours) added 28 patches on 2026-11-02, 11-05 and 11-06. We had ported 0008 and 0009 to a branch. The new ones cover four things we have open:

- Hardware cursor and scaled sizes. [0023](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/blob/main/patches/0023-core-kms-boot-commit-joiner-cursor.patch) and [0025](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/blob/main/patches/0025-adapter-kms-modes-cursor-bootmode.patch) let i915 take over the eDP pipe, write a cursor plane directly and use the plane scaler for HiDPI sizes. The joiner and 240 Hz parts are for that panel, not a 2560x1600 one.
- Backlight. The same patches call i915's `intel_backlight_set_acpi()` and add an `IODisplayParameterHandler` for the slider. It needs i915 to have found a backlight on the panel.
- Colour stages. [0030](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/blob/main/patches/0030-agdc-colour-stages-bgra-plane.patch) reports AGDC colour stages and a BGRA plane; without it CoreDisplay runs one more full-screen copy per frame.
- GPU clock floor. [0035](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/blob/main/patches/0035-core-pipe-colour-gt-floor.patch) and [0036](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/blob/main/patches/0036-accel-colour-gt-floor-numbered-marks.patch) raise the SLPC minimum frequency when frames come late. The author's numbers: 126 fps with it, 52 to 68 without.

Also useful: a per-CPU MMIO watchdog (0028, 0029) that names the register read that never finished.

Not in there: anything for the GuC GGTT top, sleep and wake, or chips other than Arrow Lake.

The pitch mismatch on 3120-wide panels ([WTLIntelGfx #14](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLIntelGfx/issues/14)) is not fixed. [0024](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/blob/main/patches/0024-accel-deferred-flip-tile4-pageoff.patch) reads `PLANE_STRIDE` and refuses the flip when it differs, so those frames take the copy path. The thread is [ArrowWhatever #2](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/issues/2).

Confidence: High that the patches contain this (diffs read), Medium that they work as described (one machine, the author's own numbers). The patches are PolyForm Noncommercial, like the tree they apply to.

## New trees built on XeGfx

- [hepengfei00/XeMac](https://github.com/hepengfei00/XeMac): 7D67 again, with an external DisplayPort screen at 4K60 HiDPI. It pins our xe branch plus ArrowWhatever 0001 to 0037 and adds [0038 to 0046](https://github.com/hepengfei00/XeMac/tree/HEAD/archive/graphics/patches): DisplayPort and AUX mapping, WindowServer framebuffer fetch, native timing published to AGDC. It credits us and keeps the notices. Screenshots and docs, no raw logs. Medium.
- [taqieallah/ThinkPad-E16-ArrowLake-macOS](https://github.com/taqieallah/ThinkPad-E16-ArrowLake-macOS): 7D51, a [diff](https://github.com/taqieallah/ThinkPad-E16-ArrowLake-macOS/blob/HEAD/patches/wtlintelgfx-e16.diff) on WTLIntelGfx 0.0.47 with a zero-copy flip, 43 to 53 fps reported. Open problem there: the CPU sits near 550 MHz. Medium.
- [change-48/XEGFX-MIGRATE](https://github.com/change-48/XEGFX-MIGRATE): the bring-up log behind #14. Findings: a `reserve_bootfb` of -28 means the GGTT allocator does not know the boot framebuffer is in use and the accelerator overwrites it; plane geometry registers only latch when `PLANE_SURF` is written; a frame at p50 24.8 ms splits into 10.84 ms in the driver and 14.10 ms after the signal. Medium.

## Scaled HiDPI, backlight and sleep on zep245's driver

[zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver) (Tiger Lake 9A49, releases 1.1.4 and 1.2.5) has an accelerated WindowServer confirmed by two outside testers in [its issue 1](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver/issues/1). Its [DESIGN.md](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver/blob/main/DESIGN.md) records how scaled sizes were made to appear ([commit c71dffc](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver/commit/c71dffcdfc0fa7b23e694b1066dfa6d6d0ea6b28)): publish `IOFBScalerInfo` and `IOFBTimingRange`, accept programmed timings, allocate the scan-out buffer larger than the panel and let a plane scaler shrink it, set `kDisplayModeValidForHiResFlag`, and put the panel EDID on the display services. Override plists alone do not add sizes. The same file has notes on the brightness parameters and on handing power-state calls to IOFramebuffer for wake. Medium, one laptop. Video decode and power numbers there are the author's own.

## Backlight leads from other laptops

- Two hybrid gaming laptops only got a working slider through the firmware's WMI brightness method (`\_SB.PC00.AWMI.WMAA`), not through the panel: [WhateverXe-RaptorLakeP](https://github.com/samranahmad09/WhateverXe-RaptorLakeP) and [NootedGreen 0326434](https://github.com/victorwitkamp/NootedGreen/commit/0326434f4c). Whether a Legion 5 15IAX10 has that method is not checked. Low for our machine.
- [WhateverXe #70](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/issues/70): a panel resets DPCD 0x720 and 0x721 after display doze, so AUX brightness has to be re-asserted.
- [WilliamAlexanda 43f626e](https://github.com/WilliamAlexanda/macOS-for-RedmiBook-Pro-15-2021/commit/43f626e): brightness capped at 18.5% because the driver's PWM period differed from the firmware's. Read the period before writing a duty.

## FreeBSD drm-kmod

For [WTLIntelGfx #4](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLIntelGfx/issues/4):

- [#315](https://github.com/freebsd/drm-kmod/issues/315) is still open, quiet since June.
- [#418](https://github.com/freebsd/drm-kmod/pull/418) is merged: GMADR init on Meteor Lake and newer, media GT1 skipped, HDCP off.
- [#468](https://github.com/freebsd/drm-kmod/pull/468) was replaced by a scatterlist fix in freebsd-src ([a6b40d7](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/commit/a6b40d746b)) and by [#534](https://github.com/freebsd/drm-kmod/pull/534) (open) for the fictitious BAR range.
- Three LinuxKPI fixes bear on hangs: [79e290d](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/commit/79e290d967) (`cancel_delayed_work_sync`, a Meteor Lake tester says it ended a sporadic hang), [2ff9182](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/commit/2ff9182d89) and [f739d9f](https://github.com/freebsd/freebsd-src/commit/f739d9f46c).

High for the state of the pull requests, Medium for the effect.
