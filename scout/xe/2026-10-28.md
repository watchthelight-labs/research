# xe: 2026-10-28 (full run, window 2026-10-27 18:11 to 2026-10-28 11:30 UTC)

Two forks from the same NullMoth `xe` lineage as XeGfx, one on our exact CPU, now have answers to our next three
problems: scaled HiDPI sizes, the slow copy into the firmware framebuffer, and the stale squares near the cursor.

## Actionable

**zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver 1.0.0: panel fitter, hardware cursor and page flips on Tiger Lake.**
[The repo](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver) tagged 1.0.0. Its framebuffer bridge
(`gpu-port/xkpi/override/src/nmx_fb_bridge.c`) runs scaled desktops such as 1680x1050 on a 2560x1600 panel by keeping
the panel's timing and giving i915 a smaller source in a drm modeset, so i915 programs the pipe's panel fitter itself.
The cursor goes through `drm_mode_cursor2_ioctl` into i915's cursor plane (256x256 ARGB, falls back to the software
cursor if the plane refuses), and frames reach the panel by a nonblocking atomic flip of the primary plane (about
1.3 ms). For us this is a design input for scaled sizes on Intel
([WTLNvidiaGfx #19](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLNvidiaGfx/issues/19)): our XeGfxIntelFB 0.7 got the panel's EDID and
the 1280x800 HiDPI mode, and stops at the scaler. Its `DESIGN.md` also says Tahoe lands on a scaled 1x mode there, not
true 2x. Licence PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. High confidence on what it does.

**zep245: the plugin's stand-in copy and invisible letters, a lead for our I-119.** `DESIGN.md` (entry 10-25):
typed letters stayed invisible until the next key in accelerated mode, because the plugin copied small
`newTiledTextureWithBytesNoCopy` buffers (512 KB or less) once and never read them again, while Core Animation
updates them in place. Our plugin comes from the same tree, and our I-119 (clear squares that trail the cursor on the
Intel desktop) looks like stale content. Medium confidence that it is the same cause.

**Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever: zero-copy GGTT flip and a deferred flip at about 60 fps (275HX, 7D67).**
Patches 0008 and 0009 ([a77d9df](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/commit/a77d9dfbf8),
[d39f908](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/commit/d39f908475), `docs/zero-copy-flip.md`) reserve three
unevictable 16,384,000-byte GGTT slots, map the compositor's IOSurface pages into one and write `PLANE_SURF_1_A`
(0x7019c), with a live switch back to the CPU copy. 2,837 flips in 200 s, no panic. Patch
[b0e2fa2](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/commit/b0e2fa2ebf) (`docs/k9-deferred-flip.md`) then lets
WindowServer submit without waiting and has a worker write `PLANE_SURF` only once every engine is idle: about 60 fps
at 2560x1600, up from about 15, with no tearing. Our frames still go through a copy into the firmware framebuffer.
Patches against XeGfx 0.0.44. High confidence.

## Notable

**ArrowWhatever's userptr panic is our I-121.** `docs/userptr-panic.md` (patch 0001) has the same panic text,
"freeing wired page ... (ref 0x1)" from `i915_gem_userptr_put_pages`, with the same cause: a pin landing between
the last unwire and the free. They fixed it with one unwire under `xg_vm_mtx` and a private hold bit; XeGfx 0.0.46
([6e3dec4](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLIntelGfx/commit/6e3dec4)) takes the wiring under the table lock only
while the count is above zero. Found independently on both sides. High confidence it is the same bug.

**ArrowWhatever patch 0010** ([58ccd9c](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever/commit/58ccd9c1ba)): 1280x800
HiDPI through `IOFBDetailedTimings`, and the same conclusion we reached: other scaled sizes need the pipe scaler.
`docs/refresh.md` adds that CVDisplayLink reported 30 Hz until the min and max pixel clock were equal. High confidence.

**zep245: NVRAM breadcrumbs made flips take about 700 ms**, the same slow firmware write we turned off to stop the
I-116 freeze. Also a SAGV PCODE request in the commit tail that never returned (they make `intel_has_sagv()` return
false) and PSR1 halving idle power (10.7 W to 5.2 W). Medium confidence for Arrow Lake.

**WhateverXe 0.6.1** ([release](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/releases/tag/0.6.1)): experimental eDP and
guarded PWM brightness for Alder Lake and Raptor Lake. A reference for backlight
([WTLNvidiaGfx #17](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLNvidiaGfx/issues/17)), not our generation.
