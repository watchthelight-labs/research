# xe, quick scout 2026-11-27

## WhateverXe 0.8.6 (notable, Medium)

[Release 0.8.6](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/releases/tag/0.8.6), for Graphics 0.4.5: new `-wex-tgl-edp-keep`
for Tiger Lake panels that go dark at the handover (keeps the firmware mode, may keep a lower colour depth); fixes for an
eDP re-link timeout after display sleep, wrong colour depth signalling when macOS reuses an eDP/DP link, an unnecessary
delay in link training on displays with extended receiver capabilities, and a video stall when interrupt retry setup
failed. Same idea as our firmware-trained link approach in XeGfxIntelFB; nothing for the Arrow Lake panel on 25G416
that blocks I-116 or I-119. Note issue [43](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/issues/43) (ADL-H keep the
firmware-trained link) as a second project arriving at keep-the-link.

## zep245/IrisXeMetal-Driver-macOS issue 1 (notable, Medium)

[Issue 1](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXeMetal-Driver-macOS/issues/1) (opened today by a new user): `trainLink()` uses
fixed 2 lanes at 4.32 Gbps and never reads DPCD lane count or rate table; a 3456x2160 4-lane eDP OLED needs more. A point
for the same wide-panel thread as our [WTLIntelGfx 14](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLIntelGfx/issues/14);
ours keeps the firmware link, so this does not hit us. Third-party report, not reproduced.
