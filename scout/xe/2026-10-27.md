## Actionable

**zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver: a working i915 and Mesa ANV driver for Iris Xe.**
[The repo](https://github.com/zep245/IrisXe-macos-driver) is a fork of NullMoth's Intel path that runs on Tiger Lake
9A49 under Sequoia 15.8.1: FreeBSD drm-kmod i915 and LinuxKPI inside the kernel (NMXGPU, NMXAccel, NMDRM), and a Metal
plugin on Mesa ANV with a translator descended from metal2vulkan. Display, cursor, brightness, PSR1, RC6 and sleep
work; idle power is 5.2 W. Tahoe is planned. That is the same shape as XeGfx, one generation older. Two parts of its
notes line up with our open problems:
- Sandboxed apps get no Vulkan device because the sandbox refuses `opendir(/dev/dri)`. They answer it with a hook in
  the plugin and a shader cache the sandbox can write to. That is our I-106 from the other side.
- Its design notes list the in-kernel crashes it hit and their causes: the thread_call group thread limit, a
  `preemption_level -1` panic from `cond_resched` inside `ggtt_pin` and `shmem_get_pages`, a TLB flush timeout and a
  machine check at the stolen memory base. Our I-116 died under heavy I/O; these are the first places to compare.

Licence is PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, so ideas only. High confidence on what it is, Medium on the mapping to our
issues.

### Actionable

**Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever: another 275HX with the 7D67 iGPU, running on XeGfx.**
[The repo](https://github.com/Mountainatic/ArrowWhatever) was created on 2026-10-25 (first named
intel-xe-lpg-igpu-macos, then ArrowBridge, ArrowWhatever since the 27th). The owner has a Core Ultra 9 275HX with an
RTX 5080 Laptop on macOS 26.7.1 and ships ten patches on our 5a19a73 (XeGfx 0.0.44), plus a port of the 0.0.35 plugin.
The parts that touch our open work:
- A zero-copy GGTT scanout path with a flip deferred until the GPU is idle (their "K9" doc). They report about 15 fps
  rising to about 60 fps at 2560x1600. That is the same slow-scanout problem we have.
- A fix for a page-lifetime race in userptr buffers (their patch 0001): the panic text is `xegfx: vm: freeing wired
  page ... (ref 0x1)` from `i915_gem_userptr_put_pages`, and a 40 minute soak ran clean afterwards. Our I-116 panic in
  N-289 sat in a TLB shootdown from the i915 free worker. The two may be one bug or two; nothing links them yet.
- A read-only host pointer check (`nvmtl_range_writable`) that came from our own tree.

The patches are PolyForm Noncommercial and the scripts and docs are MIT, so we read the patches as ideas only. There
are no raw logs in the repo, so the numbers are the owner's claim. Medium confidence. We credited the repo in the
XeGfx README and corrected the line that said only one machine had run XeGfx
([534220c](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/XeGfx/commit/534220c6cb7614a4c71380bffac97b98b4f313ef)).
