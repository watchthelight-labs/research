# xe: tester replies, 2026-10-31

On 2026-10-30 we asked 42 people who publish OpenCore EFIs for Intel 11th to 14th gen and Core Ultra machines for their iGPU details. By 05:00 EDT on 2026-10-31, 14 had answered: 12 on their own repos and 2 as probe reports in [WTLIntelGfx #3](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLIntelGfx/issues/3). Everything below is what they reported. We have not reproduced any of it.

## Probe results

| Machine | iGPU | macOS | Result | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lenovo Legion Y9000P 2022, i7-12700H | 8086:46A6 rev 0C | 26.7.1 (25G241) | stage 2 passes on 0.0.46, pipe A lit | [#3](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLIntelGfx/issues/3#issuecomment-6538047492) |
| HP Victus 15-fa0xxx, i7-12650H | 8086:46A3 rev 0C | 26.7.1 (25G241) | stages 1 to 3 pass on 0.0.38, stage 4 panics | [#3](https://github.com/watchthelight-labs/WTLIntelGfx/issues/3#issuecomment-6529881723) |

The stage 4 panic is a NULL format string in `xg_vsnprintf`, reached from `kobject_init_and_add` when `intel_detect_pch` asks LinuxKPI for the ISA bridge. The bridge's newbus device probably has no name yet, so the kobject gets a NULL name. Arrow Lake does not take this path, which is why our two machines never hit it. Confidence: Medium (backtrace and source read, not reproduced). Nothing between 0.0.39 and 0.0.46 touches it.

Two things came out of this table.

- In Discrete MUX mode the iGPU either disappears or shows up as class 0x0380 (display, other) instead of 0x0300. The XeGfx stage 2 probe only looks for subclass 00, so it would miss the A16 and the Y7000P as configured. The kexts match on base class 03 and are fine. The A16 owner found this by reading `xg_probe`. Confidence: High (code read).
- 8086:9A60 (Tiger Lake-H) and 8086:A788 (Raptor Lake-HX, the small 32 EU part) are not in the XeGfx README's ID table yet.
